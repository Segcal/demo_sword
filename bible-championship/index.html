Bible Championship (Season 2)

Overview

12 Teams per season. 3 members per team. 36 participants in total. 6 face-offs per season. 6 Champion Teams emerge

About the Show

The Bible Championship is a high-impact, team-based Bible game show designed to test knowledge, understanding, speed, and accuracy in God’s Word. It is a competitive yet faith-centered platform where teams face off in intense Bible-based challenges.
