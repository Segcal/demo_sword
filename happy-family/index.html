About the Show

The Happy Family Game Show is a family-centered entertainment platform that celebrates love, unity, teamwork, knowledge and positive family values. Designed to strengthen family relationships through exciting games and practical challenges, the show inspires stronger homes and rewards outstanding families across Nigeria.

How it Works

1.

Register Your Family

Any family member may register; father, mother, son, daughter, cousin, or any household member. Each team must consist of exactly five (5) registered members.

2.

Obtain Audition PIN

Purchase an audition pin starting from ₦3,000. Each ₦3,000 pin gives you up to three (3) audition attempts. The more pins you purchase, the more attempts you receive, increasing your chances of qualifying for the show.

3.

Start Audition

To participate in the audition, enter your audition pin to access the audition portal. Carefully read all the instructions before proceeding to begin your audition.

4.

Selection Criteria

To qualify for either the Happy Family Game Show or the Happy Family Home Play, contestants must hit the benchmark of 10 points during the audition.

5.

Multiple Entries

After completing your audition, if you are not among the top five families and still wish to participate, you may register again using a new entry, provided you still have remaining audition attempts or purchase another audition pin.

6.

Selection and invitation

The top five (5) families will be selected based on how quickly they reach 10 points. These families will receive an invitation to participate in the Happy Family Game Show. Families who also score 10 points but are not among the top five will automatically qualify for the Happy Family Home Play Quiz, where they will compete online for exciting prizes.
