National Bible Cup

Overview

The National Bible Cup is a nationwide Bible-based competition designed in a tournament format, where participants represent states of Nigeria in a structured Bible intelligence challenge.

About the Show

The National Bible Cup is Nigeria's premier state-based Bible competition, designed to celebrate knowledge of the Word of God while fostering teamwork, healthy competition, and national pride. Teams from all 36 states plus the Federal Capital Territory (Abuja) compete to be declared the Bible State of the year. Participants will be recognized at both the team level and individual level, with prizes, certificates, and official acknowledgment.

Qualification System

Open Qualification

All registered participants undergo online CBT Bible screening. Ranking is based on “accuracy” and “speed”.

State Formation

Participants are grouped into state teams and formation is based on performance and qualification results.

Competition Structure

Only 16 State Teams qualify into the main tournament stage.

Round 1 — Group Stage
  • 16 States divided into 4 groups: Group A, B, C, D
  • Each group has 4 states
  • Top 2 states from each group advance to the next round
  • Variety of Bible tasks are used to evaluate knowledge and teamwork
Round 2 — Quarterfinals (Round of 8)
  • 8 States compete head-to-head
  • Matchups arranged based on Group Stage rankings
  • Top 4 states advance
Round 3 — Semifinals (Round of 4)
  • 4 States compete for a place in the finals
  • Tasks increase in complexity and challenge
Round 4 — Finals
  • 2 States compete for the National Bible Cup
  • Winner is declared Bible State of the Year
  • Individual participants also receive cash prizes and certificates
