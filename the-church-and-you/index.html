The Church and You

Overview

Encourage understanding of church dynamics. Educate members on realistic expectations of pastors. Promote transparency, reflection, and practical advice.

About the Show

The Church and You is a premium podcast-style talk show with an audience, offering a safe, balanced, and interactive platform for Christians to discuss church experiences globally. The show allows participants to

  • Share real-life experiences, frustrations, and insights regarding their church
  • Offer advice to fellow church members
  • Provide constructive feedback to pastors and church leadership
