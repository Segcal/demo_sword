Ilorin Youth Mega Festival

10th - 12th April, 2026 Overview

The Ultimate Celebration of Nigerian Talent, Culture, and Heritage — Ilorin Youth Mega Festival 2026.

About the Show

The Ilorin Youth Mega Festival is a premier Nigerian cultural and talent festival that celebrates creativity, intelligence, and indigenous heritage. Attendees will enjoy a rich mix of music, dance, drama, art exhibitions, workshops, competitions, and interactive sessions — designed to empower talents, nurture creativity, and honor Nigeria’s rich cultural legacy.

How To Participate

1.

Select Your Participation Type

Choose how you want to be part of the festival — as a competitor, performer, exhibitor, volunteer, spectator, or sponsor. Everyone can be involved in the way that suits them.

2.

Fill Out the Registration Form

Provide your personal details, category (if applicable), and participation type. Subsections are available for specific talents or genres (e.g., dance, music, fashion, art).

3.

Make Your Payment (if required)

Complete the registration or sponsorship payment to confirm your participation.

4.

Get Your Confirmation

Receive an official confirmation via email or SMS.

5.

Prepare and Show Up

Competitors and performers should rehearse or prepare content; spectators and sponsors should plan to attend or view online.

Award Categories & Requirements

Participants and audiences can compete or vote in the following categories

+

Participants can enter to compete for awards or support contestants by voting.

+

All contestants must have a valid Festival Pass ID to participate.

+

All voters must have a SWORD Television Subscription Engagement Pin to vote.

Face of Nigeria Award — Male & Female

Celebrating confidence, beauty, and strong presence.

Requirements

  • Upload a clear, high-quality close-up facial photo
  • Image must be neat, well-lit, and visually appealing
  • Poor-quality images will be rejected
See how it works ▲
  • Approved entries are displayed in the competition gallery
  • Contestants promote their entry to gather votes
  • Winners are determined by highest number of votes

Most Indigenous Nigerian Award — Male & Female

Celebrating Nigerian culture, tradition, and identity.

Requirements

  • Dress in any Nigerian cultural attire
  • Upload a clear full-body photo
  • Outfit must represent a Nigerian ethnic group
See how it works ▲
  • Entries appear in the gallery
  • Contestants drive votes from the public
  • Winners are determined by highest number of votes

Most Intelligent Nigerian Award (1 Male & 1 Female Winner)

Testing knowledge of Nigerian culture and heritage.

Format (Live Competition)

  • Takes place during the festival (Day 2 or Final Day)
  • Conducted via mobile devices

Requirements

  • Valid Festival Pass ID
  • Access to quiz platform
See how it works ▲
  • Contestants answer 20 questions
  • Questions are randomly selected from a 200-question bank
  • Timed assessment (speed + accuracy matters)
  • Highest scoring male and female emerge winners

Note: This category is NOT based on votes

Most Creative Award — Male & Female

Showcase your creativity in any form — art, performance, craft, or skill.

Requirements

  • Upload a video (max 60 seconds)
  • Video must clearly show your creative act
  • Behind-the-scenes or process is encouraged
See how it works ▲
  • Entries are published in the gallery
  • Contestants campaign for votes
  • Winners are determined by highest number of votes
